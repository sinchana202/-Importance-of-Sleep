# Importance-of-Sleep

Sleep is an important part of physical and mental well-being.
Insufficient or disrupted sleep can contribute to tiredness and may make concentration, mood, and everyday activities more difficult.
Maintaining a consistent sleep routine can therefore be one component of a healthy lifestyle.
People interested in the mental health physical health relationship can learn more about how sleep and other lifestyle factors fit into the broader mind-body connection.
Persistent sleep difficulties may have different causes and can be discussed with an appropriate healthcare professional.
